06 - Risks already in V1's mitigation

The top three pilot risks have answers.

Reviewer queue overflow

Default SLA gentle prod at 3 days, escalation at 5 days. Pause submissions if >30% stuck >7 days. Dashboard exposes per-reviewer time-to-decision.

Secrets in published articles

9-pattern scanner runs at submit time. Reviewer sees a warning with pattern type and an 8-char preview. Weekly re-scan cron warns on already-published rows.

External link rot

Weekly link-health cron HEAD-checks every external_reference; broken links notify the reviewer. Freshness pill turns amber after the per-kind threshold (180 days for external).
